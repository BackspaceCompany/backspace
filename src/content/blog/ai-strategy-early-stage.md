The value trap in generative AI is simple: teams ship impressive demos that never connect to a measurable business outcome.

## Demo ≠ product

Demos optimize for the wow moment. Products optimize for repeat use, error recovery, and cost per successful task. If your roadmap cannot name the metric that improves when the model ships, pause.

## Three failure modes we see repeatedly

1. **Automating the wrong workflow** — high visibility, low leverage
2. **Missing human-in-the-loop** — trust collapses after the first bad answer
3. **No eval harness** — quality regresses silently after launch

## A practical filter

Ask: *If this model disappeared tomorrow, who would notice and what would break?* If the answer is "our investors in the demo room," you are in the trap.

Escape the trap by anchoring every initiative to a workflow owner, a baseline metric, and a 6-week eval window before scaling spend.
